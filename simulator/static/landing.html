Common buyer questions.

Straight answers on voice quality, legal compliance, DND scrubbing, and per-minute billing.

Does it sound robotic on Indian phone lines?
No. Archie and Aria run on low-latency Indian English voice models tuned for local names and business phrasing. Audition any voice card above or test live in the browser demo.
Will listeners know they are speaking with an AI?
Yes. Our standard opening greeting identifies the caller as an AI assistant from the start, and our system rules forbid claiming to be human.
Which languages work today?
Indian English (`en-IN`) is active and tested end to end across live WebSocket calls. Hindi and Telugu remain in beta planning and will only appear once fully verified.
How do you handle DND numbers and TRAI compliance?
Promotional campaigns require your DLT registration, 140/160 series routing through your telephony provider, DND list scrubbing before dialling, and calling hours locked to 09:00 to 21:00 IST.
Can I bring my own phone number or SIP trunk?
Yes. Vaakora supports Exotel, Plivo, Twilio, Vobiz SIP trunks, and hardware GoIP GSM SIM gateways inside the SIMs tab.
What happens if a prospect asks a lengthy or off-script question?
SmartTurn end-of-turn detection waits through natural breathing pauses so slow speakers are not cut off mid-sentence, and the agent answers multi-part questions directly from your knowledge base before pitching next steps.
How is talk time billed?
Talk time is billed per second, rounded up to the nearest 6-second pulse on connected calls. Unanswered browser or ring-only tests consume zero call minutes.
Can I test my exact script before calling real prospects?
Yes. Configure your business pitch and instructions inside the console and speak with your agent in the browser sandbox until you are satisfied.
