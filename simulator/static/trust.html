Trust, telecom rules, and data handling in plain English.

Everything below describes what our platform enforces today and what your business must register with an Indian telecom operator before running live campaigns.

Core compliance safeguards

1. TRAI & DLT obligations

Customers running promotional outbound calls in India must register as a Principal Entity on an operator DLT portal (Jio, Airtel, Vi, or BSNL), route promotional traffic via 140-series numbers (160-series for service/transactional calls), scrub lists against NCPR DND, and dial strictly between 09:00 and 21:00 IST.

2. Upfront AI & recording notice

Standard presets identify the caller as an AI assistant in the first sentence. If a listener says "take me off your list", "stop calling", or "do not call", EndCallGuardProcessor ends the call immediately after a polite acknowledgement.

3. DPDP Act 2023 & retention

Your business acts as the Data Fiduciary; Vaakora acts as the Data Processor. Public browser demo sessions are purged after 30 days. Customers can request export or deletion of stored leads and transcripts at any time via grievance@vaakora.in.

4. Security & transport encryption

All browser audio and control events stream over encrypted WebSocket (WSS/HTTPS in production). API keys stay server-side in environment configuration and are never sent to browser clients.
