Vaakora, Hyderabad, India.

1. Trial & evaluation

2. Unused wallet credits

Unused prepaid top-up credits remain valid for 12 months. Duplicate charges or accidental over-top-ups reported within 7 days (prior to consumption) are refunded to the original payment method minus payment gateway fees.

If a technical outage caused billing on failed zero-audio sessions, email the call IDs to grievance@vaakora.in for verification and credit restoration within 5 business days.
