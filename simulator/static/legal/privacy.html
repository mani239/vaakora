Privacy Policy

Vaakora, Hyderabad, India. Aligned with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. What we collect

When you run a public browser demo or submit the contact form, we store your call transcript, qualification summary, inquiry fields, and basic connection logs needed to enforce daily demo quotas.

2. Data fiduciary vs processor

For workspace campaigns, the customer organization acts as the Data Fiduciary and Vaakora acts as the Data Processor handling audio, lead records, and call logs under your instructions.

3. Retention & deletion

Public demo records are scheduled for removal after 30 days. Customers and end-listeners may request correction or erasure of personal data by writing to grievance@vaakora.in.

4. Sub-processors

Real-time audio and text turns are processed solely to deliver the active call using the sub-processors listed on our Trust & compliance page.
