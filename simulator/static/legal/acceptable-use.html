Acceptable Use Policy

Applies to all browser sandboxes and live outbound telephony campaigns on Vaakora.

Prohibited activities

  • Calling numbers without prior consent, legitimate business basis, or NCPR DND scrubbing where required.
  • Debt recovery harassment, threats, abusive language, or repeated calls after an opt-out request.
  • Political campaigning, election persuasion, or emergency service spoofing.
  • Impersonating government agencies, banks, KYC officials, or falsely claiming the AI voice is a human when asked.
  • Using voice cloning tools without documented written consent from the speaker whose voice is cloned.

Report suspected misuse immediately to grievance@vaakora.in.
